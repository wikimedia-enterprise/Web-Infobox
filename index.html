Wiki Infobox Demo

Welcome to the live demo of the Wiki Infobox System. Hover over the highlighted names below to see the dynamic component fetch live data from the Wikipedia API!

Considered the greatest sprinter of all time, Usain Bolt holds the world records in the 100 metres, 200 metres, and 4 × 100 metres relay. An eight-time Olympic gold medallist, he is the only sprinter to win Olympic 100m and 200m titles at three consecutive Olympics.

Because this tool is completely dynamic, it works seamlessly for other athletes too! Try hovering over tennis legend Serena Williams, or the most decorated Olympian of all time, Michael Phelps.

But it doesn't stop at sports! Developers can configure the tool to scrape different fields for completely different topics. For example, hover over Barack Obama, who served as the 44th president of the United States from 2009 to 2017.
